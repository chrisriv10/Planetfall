Planetfall couldn’t load

The game website is unavailable or your connection is offline. Planetfall needs an internet connection and its live multiplayer server.

Retry connection

Check your connection, then retry. You can also choose Planetfall → Retry / reload game or press Ctrl+R.

If the game opens but matchmaking cannot connect, the multiplayer server may be starting or temporarily unavailable. Retry from the game once it recovers.

Desktop guest progress stays on this computer. For Google account progress, choose Planetfall → Open browser version.
